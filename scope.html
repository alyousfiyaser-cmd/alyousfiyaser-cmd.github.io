Core Responsibilities

Executive scope across enterprise governance and execution

The scope spans Board and leadership alignment, strategic initiative oversight, performance visibility, delegated operations, capital and change governance, risk coordination, and disciplined execution, extending far beyond conventional coordination.

Executive Governance & Decision Enablement

Prepare concise, decision-ready briefs that bring together the required facts, implications, risks, options, and a practical recommendation for the Chairman or CEO.

Board & Shareholder Alignment

Serve as a central governance link across the Chairman, CEO, Board, shareholders, and business clusters, translating resolutions and executive direction into clear, accountable action.

Strategic Initiative & Action Plan Oversight

Maintain enterprise action-plan discipline across strategic initiatives by clarifying owners, milestones, dependencies, completion criteria, and follow-through.

Performance, KPI & Baseline Oversight

Monitor delivery against agreed KPIs and performance baselines, identify material variances early, and coordinate timely corrective action or executive escalation.

Delegated Operations & Approval Stewardship

Exercise formally delegated authority across operational, purchase-order, and financial approval workflows, validating policies, the applicable ALM, bank mandates, supporting evidence, and authority limits.

Capital, Project & Change Governance

Review business cases, capital approval documents, major projects, and change requests for financial and administrative completeness, scope impact, timeline exposure, and decision readiness.

Risk, Cost & Variance Visibility

Coordinate visibility of financial exposure, budget deviation, forecast risk, delivery risk, and control issues with Finance, Internal Audit, Quality, and relevant business functions.

Executive Reporting & Dashboard Governance

Provide a reliable executive line of sight across priorities, milestones, performance indicators, decisions, and unresolved exposure through concise dashboards and progress reporting.

Cross-Functional Resolution & Escalation

Act as the focal point between business functions and the Executive Office, resolve matters at source where possible, and escalate only material exceptions with context and recommendations.
