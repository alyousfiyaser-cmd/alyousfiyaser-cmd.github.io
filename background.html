Professional Foundation

Built across governance, operations, and people

My executive approach draws on formal business education, a strong foundation in people and organizational services, and continued development across the disciplines that support sound enterprise execution.

Executive and business perspective

An MBA in Human Resources, supported by a BA in English–Translation, strengthens both business judgment and clear executive communication.

People and organizational leadership

Earlier leadership experience across HR support services and consulting adds a strong understanding of people, policy, service delivery, and organizational effectiveness.

A broad management toolkit

Professional development spans project management, risk and business continuity, quality, business analysis, and finance for non-financial executives.

Interests

Beyond the core role

Technology should strengthen professional judgment, not replace it

I follow AI, automation, and technology more broadly as supporting capabilities that can improve how work is organized and executed, while keeping human judgment, accountability, and leadership at the center.

Artificial Intelligence

Following how AI can strengthen organization, visibility, analysis, and the quality of professional work — and how quickly it is reshaping the way decisions are prepared.

Practical Automation

Exploring thoughtful ways to reduce friction and improve how work moves, without replacing judgment or accountability.

Technology & Innovation

A genuine interest in technology in all its forms — smartphones, computers, smart watches, vehicles, and connected devices — and in how each new generation makes daily life and everyday tasks simpler for people.
